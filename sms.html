Text messaging terms & privacy

Missed-call text replies from the business you called

Last updated October 9, 2026

Who gets a text

When you call the business you called and nobody can answer, the call forwards to its text-back line. A recorded greeting tells you a text is coming, and you get one text from that number so you can reach the business by text. Only people who called are texted: at most one automated text per caller every 12 hours. Any other texts are the business's personal replies to your own messages. No marketing or promotional texts are sent.

Stopping texts and getting help

  • Reply STOP at any time and you won't be texted from that number again.
  • Reply HELP for help, or call the business back.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency depends on your conversation.
  • Carriers aren't liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

What is collected and why

Your phone number, the time of your call, any voicemail you leave and the texts you send. They're used only to answer your call and continue the conversation you started.

Sharing

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party. Information is shared only with the service providers that deliver the calls and texts (Twilio), and only to deliver them.

How long it's kept

Messages and voicemails are kept while the business uses this service and deleted within 30 days after it ends, or sooner on request.
