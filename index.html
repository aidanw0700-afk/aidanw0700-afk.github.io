What I set up

Missed-call text-back

When a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text within a minute: "Sorry we missed you, what can we help with?" Their reply comes straight to your phone.

Review requests

After a job, the customer gets a short email asking for a Google review. More reviews help more people find you.

Fast replies to web leads

Someone fills out your website form and gets an answer right away, not hours later, while they're still deciding who to call.

Policies

Refunds and cancellation

If the setup isn't delivered and working as agreed within 14 days, the setup fee is refunded in full. For anything that sends texts, the 14 days start once the phone carriers approve your business for texting (a required registration that usually takes a few days to a few weeks); if they don't approve it, you get a full refund. No monthly fee is charged until it's live. Monthly service can be cancelled any time by email; it stops at the end of the current paid month, and partial months aren't refunded.

Terms

Each project's scope and price are agreed in writing before work starts. You keep ownership of your accounts, phone numbers and customer data. Messages sent on your behalf follow your approved wording and the law, including opt-out ("Reply STOP") on every text. Service is provided as-is; my liability is limited to the fees you paid in the last 3 months.

Privacy

I only handle your customers' contact details to run the automations you asked for. They are never sold or shared, and they're deleted when the service ends. Payments are processed by Stripe; I never see or store card numbers.
